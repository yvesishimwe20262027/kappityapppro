Essential road rules

Review these basics and confirm local requirements in the current Rwanda Highway Code.

Speed and control

Obey posted speed limits and reduce speed for rain, poor visibility, traffic, pedestrians, roadworks, and sharp bends. A speed limit is a maximum, not a target.

Seat belts and helmets

Wear your seat belt and ensure passengers use required restraints. Motorcycle riders and passengers should wear approved, properly fastened helmets.

Intersections

Approach carefully, obey traffic lights and signs, and give way as required. Never assume another road user has seen you.

Pedestrians

Watch for pedestrians, especially at crossings, schools, bus stops, and built-up areas. Slow down and stop when required to let pedestrians cross safely.

Overtaking

Overtake only when legal and clearly safe. Do not overtake where visibility is restricted or signs/markings prohibit it; return to your lane without cutting in.

Following distance

Leave enough space to stop safely. Increase the gap in rain, on loose surfaces, at night, or when carrying a heavy load.

Phones and alcohol

Do not drive while impaired, tired, or distracted. Put the phone away and pull over safely before handling it.

Breakdown or emergency

Move out of traffic if possible, make yourself visible, and warn approaching traffic when safe. Contact the appropriate emergency or roadside service.
